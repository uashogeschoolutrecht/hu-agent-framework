# HU Privacy And Personal Data Baseline

This is scoped privacy guidance for agent-assisted work. It is not legal advice and should be re-evaluated when the framework scales or the applicable HU policy changes.

- Establish and document a lawful basis before processing personal data.
- Treat special-category data as restricted and process it only when a valid exception applies.
- Consider a privacy impact assessment for large-scale special-category processing, systematic monitoring, profiling, or automated decision-making with legal or similarly significant effects.
- Account for data-subject rights, including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection where applicable.
- Keep personal data no longer than necessary and record retention periods.
- Escalate suspected data breaches through the current HU incident process; do not rely on a copied contact detail or an unverified deadline.

Agents should classify unclear personal-data situations as high risk and recommend human privacy review.
